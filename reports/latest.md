# Hiring Pulse — 2026-11-23

Startup hiring this week, measured from the public job boards (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, Recruitee and Personio) of 21,598 companies, read every day. Figures as of 23 November 2026. 284 tracked companies are excluded from the benchmark (173 staffing and recruiting agencies, 63 large employers, 29 vendor test boards, 9 hospitals, universities and public bodies, 8 franchise networks, 1 board above the startup size limit and 1 other exclusion); they stay in the daily feed. The rule is in the methodology.

## Key findings

- **Open roles stand at 465,614 across the 21,598 companies tracked this week.** Engineering is the largest function with 92,485 openings (19.9%), then Sales (10.9%) and Operations (10.9%).
  - *For analysts: the size of startup hiring demand by function, re-measured from 21,598 first-party boards every day.*
- **More companies are cutting than growing: 13.4% of those with 14+ days of history are contracting, 7.3% accelerating.** Across the 648 companies with 14+ days of daily history, open roles are +0.9% against 16 days earlier (1,895 opened, 1,800 closed). By industry, Education is growing fastest (+19.5%) and Industrials slowest (−2.5%).
  - *For sales teams: the accelerating companies are opening budgets now; the daily feed names them, with the roles they added.*
- **Aleph Lab added 43 open roles in 16 days, more than any other company; Inversion Space cut the most (−34).** Also expanding: Fivetran (+37, 196 open), Legora (+28, 344 open); also cutting: Astranis (−17, 168 open), Deepgram (−17, 52 open).
  - *For revenue teams: a company that grows its open roles by a third is about to buy tools, payroll and office space. The earliest public budget signal.*
- **A startup role stays open a median of 64.2 days before it is filled or withdrawn; Product roles take 85 days, Clinical 35.** Measured on 1,746 postings that closed in the last 16 days at 338 companies.
  - *For talent teams: a time-to-fill benchmark by function, industry and company size, against companies like yours.*
- **AI/ML roles are 3.2% of all openings (14,876 roles), but at 10x Team and Reonic they are over 90% of the board.** Heaviest AI/ML hirers among boards with 10+ roles: 10x Team (94.0% of its openings), Reonic (93.3% of its openings).
  - *For investors: the AI share of each company's board, day by day, separates AI-native teams from AI-washed decks.*
- **16.5% of open roles are remote, and the typical company offers none.** 531 of the 8,218 companies with 10 or more openings are fully remote.
  - *For employer-brand and compensation teams: remote is the exception, which makes it a differentiator in the posting.*

The growth, days-to-fill and movers figures cover the 648 companies with 14+ days of daily history; the other 20,950 tracked companies join them as their history accumulates, most from the edition of 2026-12-07. Next edition: Monday 2026-11-30, 07:00 UTC.

## About the numbers

Source: daily reads of the public job boards (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, Recruitee and Personio) of 21,598 companies. Every figure is first-party: a role is counted once, from the company's own board, and a closure is observed the day the posting disappears, not inferred. Job titles are mapped to functions, seniority and the AI/ML flag with one fixed taxonomy, so editions are comparable week to week. Method, definitions and the daily accuracy checks: [hiring.agilityy.com/methodology](https://hiring.agilityy.com/methodology/). Every edition: [hiring.agilityy.com](https://hiring.agilityy.com).
