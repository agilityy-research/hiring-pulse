# Hiring Pulse — 2026-11-20

Baseline study: a one-off 14-day readout on 639 Y Combinator companies whose public job boards (Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby) were read daily from 2026-11-06 to 2026-11-20, before the full universe had any history. It is the first look at growth, roles opened and closed, days-to-fill and the movers; the weekly series starts with the Monday editions.

- Open roles: **10,886** (+137 vs window start, 1.3%)
- Roles opened / closed: 1,768 / 1,631
- Companies accelerating / contracting: 6.9% / 11.1%
- Median days-to-fill: 65 (from 1,584 closures at 319 companies; 47 postings older than a year excluded)
- Median remote share: 20.0%
- AI/ML roles: **579** (5.3% of open roles)
- Seniority mix: Entry 5.1%, Mid 55.4%, Senior 14.0%, Staff/Principal 8.2%, Manager 9.9%, Director 3.1%, Executive 4.2%

## About the numbers

Source: daily reads of the public job boards (Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby) of 639 Y Combinator companies, 2026-11-06 to 2026-11-20. Every figure is first-party: a role is counted once, from the company's own board, and a closure is observed the day the posting disappears, not inferred.
