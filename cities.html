How these numbers are made

What stands behind each figure

One role, one city

A role counts once, in the first city its posting names. A posting listing five offices counts in the first.

Names cleaned

Spellings of one city are merged (NYC and New York City, München and Munich). A city with no country takes the country most of its roles name.

Startups only

Employers with more than 1,000 open roles, staffing agencies and franchises are left out, as in the weekly report.

Refreshed weekly

Recomputed every Monday from that morning's read of every board. Methodology.
