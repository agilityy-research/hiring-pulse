How to read these numbers

Advertised pay, counted carefully

What the posting says

The range the employer publishes, not offers or pay received. Each role counts at the midpoint of its range, or at the one figure given.

No mixing

Currencies and periods are never converted into each other: salaries, hourly wages and monthly pay are separate figures.

Duplicates count once

The same title and range posted many times by one company counts once (once per city in the city figures).

Minimum sample

A figure needs {{ m.pay.minRoles }} roles from {{ m.pay.minCompanies }} companies, and no company may supply more than half of it. Below that, the cell is left blank.

{{ cov.withPayT }} of {{ cov.rolesT }} open roles ({{ cov.payPct }}%) advertise pay; {{ cov.payRejectedT }} ranges outside plausible bounds for their period (a bonus or a budget read as pay) are dropped. Pay is published mostly by US employers on Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby; SmartRecruiters' job lists and Personio never carry it, so this page describes the companies that publish pay, mostly in the US. Seniority comes from words in the job title. Methodology.
