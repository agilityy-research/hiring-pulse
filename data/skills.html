One vocabulary, every description

Each job description is matched against a fixed list of about 480 terms (programming languages, frameworks, cloud and data platforms, business tools, methods, certifications, licences and spoken languages). Short or ambiguous names (Go, R, C) match only in their exact form, so "go to market" is not Go. A skill counts once per description. SmartRecruiters roles (about a third of all open roles) are not in these shares: their descriptions are on each posting's own page, which we do not read yet. Neither are other roles whose posting has no description, most of them on Personio; {{ s.coveragePct }}% of the roles with a description are tagged this week. Methodology.
