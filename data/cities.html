What stands behind each figure

One role, one city

A role counts once, in the first city its posting names. A posting listing five offices counts in the first.

Names cleaned

Spellings of one city are merged (NYC and New York City, München and Munich). A city with no country takes the country most of its roles name.

Startups only

Employers with more than 1,000 open roles, staffing agencies, franchises and test boards are left out, as in the weekly report.

Updated weekly

Recomputed from the latest daily read of every board; the date at the top is that read. Methodology.
